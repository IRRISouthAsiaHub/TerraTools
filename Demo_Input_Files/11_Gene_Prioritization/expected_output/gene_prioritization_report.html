Methods

  • Score. S(g) = [Σ wi · E(g,i) / Σ wi] × 100 over the evidence categories available for the gene. Weights: Known gene-trait association 30%, Functional relevance to the trait 25%, Pathway relevance 20%, Ortholog evidence 15%, Literature support 10%.
  • Trait words. direct: yield, grain yield, productivity; related: grain filling, tillering, tiller, biomass, harvest index, sink, source, panicle, seed set.
  • Sources. Ensembl Plants REST, Gramene, Plant Reactome, NCBI E-utilities (PubMed, Gene) and, for rice, Oryzabase (curated genes keyed by RAP-DB ID). Each evidence item lists its source and link; retrieval dates are in the Excel workbook.
